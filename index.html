About me

Hello, I am Abdullah Al Noman. I graduated in Water Resources Engineering from BUET in 2026, and I now work as a Research Associate in the same department.

I like to use data and models to understand rivers, floods and climate. I combine machine learning, spatial analysis and hydrodynamic modeling to work on climate and water management problems. I build models in HEC-RAS and HEC-HMS, and I use Python and ArcGIS for data analysis.

My undergraduate thesis brought these skills together. In it, I compared machine learning and deep learning models for flood forecasting in the Ganges River Basin. If you would like to talk about this work, please feel free to contact me.
